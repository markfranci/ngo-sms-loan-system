Skip Logic Help

Skip logic lets you branch the survey based on a member's answer.
• Skip Condition: The answer that triggers the skip (e.g. "No", "Mama Samaki"). Case-insensitive.
• Skip To Question #: The question number to jump to. Use 0 to end the survey immediately.
• If the answer does not match the condition, the survey proceeds to the next question normally.
